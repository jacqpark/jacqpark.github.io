Tilly 1985

Protection, two ways

Comforting

A shelter against danger, such as a powerful friend, a large insurance policy or a sturdy roof.

Ominous

A racket in which a local strongman makes merchants pay to avoid damage he himself threatens to deliver.

"Someone who produces both the danger and, at a price, the shield against it is a racketeer."

When the threats a government guards against are imaginary or of its own making, the government runs a protection racket.

Tilly 1985 · Violence and government

From bandits to kings

Bandits and pirates▶Tax collectors▶Regional powerholders▶Professional soldiers▶Kings
Blurred lines

Kings commissioned privateers and hired bandits to raid their enemies. Demobilized troops became bandits and demobilized ships became pirates.

Disarming the great

The Tudors broke up the lords' private armies and razed their fortresses. In France, Richelieu began the great disarmament in the 1620s.

Indirect rule

No monarch could govern a large population by armed force alone. Rulers leaned on local magnates, who were also their likeliest rivals.

The line between legitimate and illegitimate violence came clear only slowly, as states made their armed forces unified and permanent.

Tilly 1985

How war builds a state

  1. A lord makes war well enough to dominate a substantial territory.
  2. War needs men, arms, food and money, so he extracts them from the population.
  3. Extraction means neutralizing local rivals and building tax offices, police and courts. That is statemaking.
  4. Classes that lend money or help keep order receive protection in return.

National states grew as a by-product of rulers' pursuit of war, and none of those rulers foresaw it.

Group exercise 01

Argue with Tilly

Survivors only

Tilly notes that fewer states came out of each large war than went in. An account built on the survivors may be selecting on the outcome.

Cases that copy

Rivals imitated the winners, as when Peter the Great rebuilt Russia on Dutch examples. How independent are these cases?

Which type of study?

Tilly calls his essay history as a source of hypotheses. Where does that put it among Lijphart's six types?

"If summarizing European warmaking and statemaking is like skating on thin ice, extrapolating that summary to the contemporary world resembles walking on water."

Hanson and Sigman 2021

The capacity to do what?

◀ NarrowBroad ▶
Too narrow

Stateness

Only the monopoly on the legitimate use of force. It misses much of what modern states are expected to do.

Hanson and Sigman

Core functions

Defense against external threats, internal order, basic infrastructure for the economy, and revenue extraction.

Too broad

Every function

Justice, public services, economic management and more. It risks measuring political priorities instead of ability.

Capacity is kept apart from regime type and from policy choices. A highly capable state can still choose to do little.

Hanson and Sigman 2021

Three dimensions

Extractive

Raising revenue. It takes reach into the population, good records, trustworthy agents and enforcement.

Measured by
Taxes as a share of GDP, income and trade taxes as shares of all taxes
Coercive

Holding enough force to defend borders, keep order and enforce the law across the territory.

Measured by
Military spending and personnel, police, territory under state authority
Administrative

Making policy, delivering services and regulating commerce through capable agents and reliable information.

Measured by
Bureaucratic quality, census frequency, statistical capacity

Tilly's extraction and warmaking reappear here as dimensions you can measure.

Hanson and Sigman 2021 · Measurement

Hanson and Sigman 2021 · Validity

Does the measure work?

Face validity

In 2015 Singapore ranks in the top 25, so the measure picks up more than democracy. Somalia, Yemen and the Central African Republic sit near the bottom.

Convergent validity

Capacity correlates at .91 with the World Bank's government effectiveness index and at −.88 with the fragile states index.

Discriminant validity

Its correlation with log GDP per capita is .79. The two are close, and still distinct.

Construct validity

Researchers sent undeliverable letters to 159 countries. One point more Capacity means about 21 percentage points more letters returned, and 69 days faster.

Capacity in 1960 also predicts 2010. One point higher meant about 12 fewer infant deaths per 1,000 and 5.3 more years of life expectancy, holding income, democracy and taxes constant.

In 1982 Tilly picked Iraq as his example of an oil-exporting merchant state. Does its fall bear out his warning?

Watch out

Argue with the measure

One dimension

Only the one-dimensional model converged. That fits dimensions that are entwined, and it also fits indicators too noisy to pull apart.

Expert ratings

Several indicators are expert scores. The authors concede that bureaucratic quality ratings may reflect analysts' views of economic outcomes.

National averages

One score per country and year hides variation inside a state, such as a capital that works and a periphery that does not.
